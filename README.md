# Diwali Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Diwali Sales dataset using Python. The objective is to understand customer purchasing behavior during the festive season and uncover valuable business insights through data cleaning, visualization, and analysis.

By analyzing customer demographics, purchasing patterns, and product preferences, businesses can make data-driven decisions to improve marketing strategies, inventory planning, and customer targeting.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Explore customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Analyze sales trends across different states, age groups, occupations, and product categories.
- Generate actionable business insights to support decision-making.

---

## 📊 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning
- Removed null values.
- Dropped irrelevant columns.
- Checked data types and corrected inconsistencies.
- Prepared the dataset for analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Gender-wise analysis
- Age group analysis
- State-wise sales analysis
- Marital status analysis
- Occupation analysis
- Product category analysis

### 3. Data Visualization
Created various visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn to identify patterns and trends within the data.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👩 Gender Analysis
- Female customers contributed significantly more sales compared to male customers.

### 🎂 Age Group Analysis
- Customers aged between **26–35 years** were the most active buyers.

### 🌍 State Analysis
- Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka generated the highest sales and order volumes.

### 💍 Marital Status Analysis
- Married women were among the most valuable customer segments.

### 💼 Occupation Analysis
- Customers working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors contributed heavily to sales.

### 🛍️ Product Category Analysis
- Certain product categories attracted a larger share of customer spending, indicating strong customer demand during the festive season.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on women aged 26–35.
- Design personalized offers for married customers.
- Increase inventory availability in high-performing states.
- Prioritize promotional efforts for top-selling product categories.
- Use customer demographic insights for targeted advertising.

---

## 📈 Conclusion

The analysis reveals that customer demographics play a significant role in purchasing behavior during the Diwali season. Female customers, particularly married women aged 26–35 from states such as Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka, represent a key customer segment. These insights can help businesses optimize marketing efforts, improve customer engagement, and drive higher sales during festive periods.
